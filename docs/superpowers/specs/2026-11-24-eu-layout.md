# Emergency Unit (EU) layout: "Observe in EU": research and plan

Research only, 24 Nov 2026. **Nothing here is built.** Repo state: branch `claude/clinic-line-doctor`, worktree `admin-clinics-harmonize-efe20d`, latest ADR 0532, latest migration 0305.

## In plain words

1. Today, "Observe in EU" is only a choice in the doctor's Plan. Nothing happens after it: no place, no bed, no clock. The visit just stays open.
2. MedSync already has a ward builder: you draw a ward, its beds, oxygen points and the resus trolley, publish it, and a live bed board shows who is where. The hospital's ward list even has a type called "Emergency observation" already.
3. My recommendation: **an EU is a ward of that type, drawn in the same builder**. We do not build a second, separate drawing tool. We add two kinds of space the builder lacks: a **chair** and a **resus space**. Trolleys, observation beds and isolation rooms already exist.
4. A hospital can have **several EUs**: one at the OPD and acute bays on medical wards. Each is its own EU with its own name, place and board.
5. When the doctor chooses "Observe in EU", they pick which EU and which free space on its plan, exactly like picking a ward bed. They see free and busy spaces, never other patients' names.
6. The visit stays open while the patient is in the EU. The EU stay gets its own clock: time since arrival at hospital, and time in the EU. A stay over a limit you choose (24 hours is the usual international figure) is listed under "Needs attention". Nobody is moved automatically.
7. From the EU the patient goes to a ward (admit), home, another hospital, or theatre. If admitted while still waiting for a ward bed, they stay on their EU trolley until the ward confirms arrival.
8. EU nurses and doctors get a **tracking board**: every space, who is in it, triage level, how long they have been there, obs due, results ready, and who is waiting for a ward bed.
9. This needs new database changes that you apply yourself (GATED), a feature switch per hospital, and about six short decision records (ADRs).
10. The decisions you need to make are listed at the end, each with my recommendation.

## 2. The real world

Tags: [V] means the primary source was read. [S] means it came from a search summary or a secondary source. [U] means it could not be verified.

### 2.1 How emergency units are laid out and run

- **[V] Three streams, NHS HBN 15-01 (2013).** Patients are streamed into acute (hot), ambulatory (warm) and minors (cold) zones, with step-up and step-down between them.
  - The areas it lists include:
    - separate walk-in and ambulance entrances, reception and waiting
    - rapid assessment and treatment
    - a resuscitation area and a decontamination room
    - a clinical decision unit (CDU)
    - children's facilities
  - It asks for negative-pressure isolation next to the front door.
  - A CDU holds patients "for a number of hours", to in-patient ward standards. Reclining chairs may replace beds. HBN sets no hour cap.
  - https://www.england.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/15_01final3_v3.pdf
- **[V] ACEP on ED observation units.**
  - Patients are expected to need 6–24 h; the typical stay is about 15 h, and 70–80% go home.
  - Patients are "not expected to spend more than 24 hours" there.
  - Each unit's protocol should set the maximum allowed length of stay.
  - https://acep.org/siteassets/uploads/uploaded-files/acep/clinical-and-practice-management/resources/observation/state-of-the-art---observation-units-in-the-ed-0511.pdf
- **[S]** NHS England EEMAC describes the CDU as a stay of "up to 24 hours" between the ED and a ward: https://www.england.nhs.uk/long-read/extended-emergency-medicine-ambulatory-care-eemac-operating-principles/
- **[S]** Queensland caps ED short-stay at 24–36 h: https://www.health.qld.gov.au/system-governance/policies-standards/guidelines/emergency-dept-short-term
- **[V] WHO Resuscitation Area Designation (2023).** Resuscitation areas are hubs for life-saving interventions in the emergency unit, and sometimes on wards. This supports ward-attached acute bays. https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/resuscitation-area-designation
- **[S]** The WHO Emergency Care Toolkit covers BEC, the three-tier IITT triage tool, and the Emergency Care Checklist: https://www.who.int/teams/integrated-health-services/clinical-services-and-systems/emergency-and-critical-care/emergency-care-toolkit

## 3. Proposed design

### 3.1 An EU is a ward of type `emergency_observation` in the existing builder

**Recommendation: no new model.** An EU is a `hospital_wards` row with `ward_type = 'emergency_observation'`, drawn in the same builder and published with the same `ward_layout_publish`. It also uses the same `hospital_beds`, `bed_assignments`, `bed_state_events`, live channel, RLS and audit.

Why:
- **The type already exists** (services-hub.ts:290, 0080 CHECK).
- The geometry, checks, reconcile, occupancy guards and picker are hard-won and signed off. A copy would drift.
- `bed_assignments` enforces one open assignment per patient across the whole hospital. That is exactly the safety property we want: a patient cannot be both on an EU trolley and silently in a ward bed. A separate EU model would lose it.
- The board already reads by ward, so each EU gets its board for free.

What differs for an EU, as additions and not forks:
1. **Two new space types** in `BED_TYPES`:
   - `chair` ("Chair", about 2×2 cells), for fit-to-sit ambulatory patients and HBN's reclining CDU chairs.
   - `resus` ("Resus space", about 3×4 cells, like ICU), a space equipped for resuscitation.
   - The existing types fill the rest of the space list in 3.2.
   - This needs a GATED migration to widen the `hospital_beds_bed_type_check` constraint. `asBedType()` (line 670) falls back for unknown values.
2. **EU layout checks.** These are warnings only, like ADR 0196:
   - every resus space has O₂, suction and a monitor
   - a resus trolley fixture is within reach
   - at least one isolation room, or a warning
   - children's spaces kept apart when `age_group` is mixed (the Seed finding)
   - Add `emergency_observation` to the types that get the monitor check only for resus spaces.
3. **A template** in `lib/wards/presets.ts`, "Emergency unit": a resus bay (2 resus spaces), a trolley bay, a chair row, one isolation room, a station and a resus trolley. The admin edits it from there.
4. **`admittingWards()` excludes EU wards** (and the Plan's ward chips with it). EU wards are listed only under "Observe in EU".

The alternative considered was its own model, an `eu_spaces` table and a separate editor. It was rejected because it duplicates around 1,200 lines of geometry and checks, a publish function and a board. It would also break the hospital-wide one-patient-one-place rule.

### 3.2 EU space types and their states

| Space        | Builder representation                   | Typical use                                     |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Resus space  | new bed type `resus`, inside a bay or room named "Resus" | Red / L1; links to the resus desk |
| Trolley      | existing `couch` ("Couch or trolley")    | majors, undifferentiated sick                   |
| Chair        | new bed type `chair`                     | ambulatory / minors / fit-to-sit, obs in a chair |
| Observation bed | existing `standard`                   | the short-stay / CDU part                       |
| Isolation    | any space inside a room of kind `isolation` (with `negativePressure` / `anteroom` features) | infectious, pre-arrival "prepare isolation" flag |

**States:** reuse the six board states unchanged (Occupied, Arriving, Free, Cleaning, Reserved, Out), with one generalisation:
- "Arriving" currently means an open assignment with `admission_id` set and no `arrived_at`.
- It should also mean an assignment made for an **EU stay** with no arrival yet.
- One rule, both cases.

**Clocks**, shown as text. They are timers, never colours:
- **In hospital:** from the intake's arrival (`patient_intakes`, the clinic floor's `arrivedAt`). This is the department clock and the MoH 2/8/24 h indicators count from it.
- **In EU:** from the EU stay's start (the decision to observe).
- **In this space:** from the bed assignment's `started_at`.
- **Waiting for a ward bed:** from the admission's `decided_at`, while boarding.

Per AGENTS.md, red, amber and green are for the patient's clinical state only. An over-limit stay is a **workflow** state, so it shows as a square `Tag` ("Over 24 h") and on the Needs attention list, never in amber or red. The patient's NEWS2 or level keeps its own clinical colour.

### 3.3 The EU stay record

A small new table, `eu_stays` (GATED migration). It mirrors `admissions` but **does not close the encounter**, as ADR 0197 §2 requires.

- Columns:
  - `id`, `hospital_id`, `patient_user_id`, `encounter_id` (the open source visit)
  - `ward_id` (the EU)
  - `decided_by`, `decided_at`
  - `status`: `awaiting_space | in_space | ended`
  - `space_wait_reason`: reuse `no_free_bed | extra_bed | floor | other`
  - `arrived_at`, `arrived_by`
  - `responsible_user_id` (rota-dependent; defaults to the deciding doctor)
  - `ended_at`, `ended_by`
  - `end_kind`: `admitted | home | referred_out | transferred | theatre | died | left_before_completion | left_against_advice`
  - `admission_id` (when admitted)
- **One open stay per patient** (a unique partial index). Kept for good and only ever ends, like the bed-assignment guard. Audited.
- **Why a table and not just the bed assignment:**
  - a move from resus to a trolley ends one assignment and starts another, but the EU clock must not reset
  - "awaiting space" (a full EU, a patient on the floor) needs a row with no assignment at all
  - the MoH indicators need the outcome

### 3.4 Several EUs per hospital

- Each EU is its own ward row with its own name, layout, board and live topic. The data model has no limit.
- **Placement** reuses `hospital_places`: OPD block for "OPD Emergency Unit", Ward 4 wing for "Ward 4 acute bay", lower-Mulago A&E building for "A&E Resus and Shock". Department is `EMERGENCY` (or Internal Medicine for a ward-attached bay). `sex` and `age_group` work as on wards (for example a "Paediatric EU").
- **Naming:** the admin names it. The picker shows name, place and counts, for example "OPD Emergency Unit · OPD block · 3 trolleys, 1 resus free".
- **Ward-attached acute bays** (recommended): model the bay as **its own EU ward**, placed in the same wing as its parent ward, with an optional `parent_ward_id` for display.
  - The alternative is marking some beds inside a general ward as EU spaces.
  - That is rejected because one board would then mix inpatients and observation patients with different clocks and different "who may act" rules. Counts and the picker are per ward, so it would need per-bed filters everywhere.

### 3.5 Picking an EU and a space: the Plan and the close

- **Plan** (`plan-step.tsx`): choosing "Observe in EU" shows chips for the hospital's EU wards, with counts from `wardBedCounts()`. The draft records `euWardId`, like the existing `ward` / `wardId` for Admit.
- **Commit:** ADR 0197 makes "observe" not a closure, so the close dialog's footer becomes **"Send to EU"** instead of a disabled close.
  - It calls a new SQL function `observe_in_eu(p jsonb)`, mirroring `admit_from_encounter`:
    - check the encounter is `in_progress`
    - check the EU ward is active and of type `emergency_observation`
    - check the space is free (reuse `admission_free_bed`)
    - insert the `eu_stays` row and, if a space was chosen, a `bed_assignments` row tagged with `eu_stay_id`, which shows as Arriving
    - add an encounter event: "Sent to OPD EU, trolley T3"
  - **The encounter stays open.**
  - The function goes through `ha-complete-encounter` or a sibling Edge Function, using the service role and no client write.
- **The space picker** reuses `wardBeds()` and `PlanView` exactly as `AdmitFields` does. It reads the board with `patients: "none"`, so the doctor sees states and services only, **never another patient's name** (ADR 0199 §7). The need filters come from `freeByNeed()`. A "chair" or "resus" need may be added to `NEEDS`.
- **No free space:** the doctor chooses a wait reason (as for admission). The EU board lists the patient under "Waiting for a space". This makes corridor and floor care visible instead of hiding it, which matters at Mulago.
- **Arrival:** EU staff mark "In the EU", which sets `arrived_at`. Arriving becomes Occupied, as in ADR 0199 §4.

### 3.6 Leaving the EU

All of these run on the **still-open source encounter**:

- **Home / refer / transfer / died / left:** the existing close dialog, now enabled for an EU stay. One transaction:
  - closes the encounter (as today)
  - ends the bed assignment with a reason
  - ends the stay with `end_kind`
- **Admit to a ward (boarding):** the hard case.
  - `admit_from_encounter` refuses a patient who already has a bed (0091:245), and `bed_assignments_one_open_per_patient` (0090:181) forbids holding an EU trolley *and* an allocated ward bed (Arriving) at once.
  - Proposed change (GATED migration plus a redefined function):
    1. Replace the per-patient unique index with two: one open **Occupied** assignment per patient (`arrived_at` set, or no admission or stay), and one open **Arriving** assignment per patient.
    2. In `admit_from_encounter`, when the patient's open assignment is in an EU ward, allow it. The admission is created, and the stay records `admission_id` with status "boarding" (shown as a derived word, not a new column).
    3. In `confirm_bed_arrival`, end the EU assignment and the EU stay (`end_kind = 'admitted'`) **in the same transaction** that marks arrival.
  - The EU board then shows "Admitted · Medical Ward 2 · waiting 3 h 10 min", with the boarding clock.
- **Record gap while boarding:** `admit_from_encounter` completes the source encounter, and the inpatient encounter opens only at arrival (ADR 0200). Obs and drugs given to a boarder on the EU trolley would have no open encounter. This needs a decision: see D6.
- **Theatre:** I found no theatre model in MedSync. It is a resus disposition word only. Treat "theatre" as an `end_kind` with a destination text until a theatre module exists.
- **From resus:** a resus episode in an EU resus space keeps using the resus desk. The episode's `boarding` phase and destination remain the resus desk's. The EU board links to the episode and does not duplicate it.

### 3.7 How the clinic floor and triage link in

- **Clinic floor** (ADR 0532: the doctor's line opens the encounter): "Send to EU" from that encounter ends the floor visit with a new outcome, `eu` ("Observed in EU"), added to `Outcome` in `lib/clinic-floor/model.ts:72`. It is recorded as a `finish` event with `detail = 'eu'`. No name goes into the event.
- **Triage pop-up** (ADR 0531): "Send to" lists doctors today. **Proposed:** for L1/L2, or a red-flag pre-arrival, add "Send to EU resus" to that list. It reserves or allocates a resus space through the same function with no doctor decision yet, as an `eu_stay` with `decided_by` = the nurse.
  - **This is a workflow, not a clinical decision**, but it changes who may start an EU stay. It needs owner and CSO sign-off (D4).
- **Pre-arrival:** an `emergency_pre_arrivals` row with the isolation hint can **Reserve** an isolation space with an existing `bed_state_events` reserved state and a reason. No new mechanism is needed.
- **Resus:** `resus_bay_profiles.bay_label` is free text. Add an optional `bed_id`, so the defibrillator profile belongs to the drawn resus space. `hospital_care_units` (`ed_obs`) is a parallel unit list: link a care unit to its ward (`ward_id`), and do not create a third list (D7).

### 3.8 The EU tracking board (EU staff)

Route: an EU tab on the existing `app/nurse/beds` board, the hospital-admin ward board, and a doctor view. It is a new **list view** beside the existing plan view, from the same `loadBedBoard()` plus stays.

Columns, in this order:
1. space (and "waiting for a space")
2. patient: name and MRN for EU staff (`patients: "names"`, behind `mayWorkBeds` and an audited PHI read like the resus board)
3. triage level in the hospital's own words
4. clocks: in hospital, in EU, in space, boarding
5. responsible doctor and nurse (from the rota)
6. next obs due, from the Plan's obs frequency and the last observation
7. results ready (released labs, as the clinic floor's `resultsReady`)
8. status word: Arriving, In EU, Review due, Admitted · waiting for ward bed, Ready to go
9. resus link

**Needs attention**, a section (ADR 0199 §6 pattern):
- stay over the limit (D2)
- Arriving over 1 h
- obs overdue
- boarding over 12 h from the decision to admit (the NHS trolley-wait measure)
- waiting for a space

Nothing moves automatically.

**Privacy:**
- The waiting-room screen never shows the EU.
- Doctors outside the EU pick spaces with `patients: "none"`.
- Live broadcasts stay free of patient data.

### 3.10 Risks

1. **The boarding record gap (D6).** Care given to an admitted patient still on an EU trolley has no open encounter. This is the biggest clinical-record risk.
2. **Two or three unit lists.** These are `hospital_wards` (EU type) and `hospital_care_units` (`ed_obs`), plus the free-text `resus_bay_profiles.bay_label`. Without linking them, the resus desk and the EU board will disagree about where a patient is.
3. **The per-patient index change touches live admissions.** It is a GATED migration on a table in production use. The PGlite tests must prove no existing row becomes invalid.
4. **Data-entry load in a crowded EU.** At about 48,000 trauma patients a year, if marking arrival or moving a space takes more than a tap, staff will skip it and the board will lie. Keep each action one tap, with offline queueing like the clinic floor triage (ADR 0497).
5. **Clocks read as alarms.** A time-in-EU Tag must not look like a clinical warning (colour rule). It also must not push early discharge: it is a flag for review, not a target.
6. **Triage scale mismatch.** UTAT has three categories, MedSync has five levels. The board must use the hospital's configured words, or staff will misread "L3".
7. **EU wards appearing as admitting wards** today. `admittingWards()` has no type filter, so an admin who creates an "Emergency observation" ward now gets it offered under Admit. That is a small fix, but it is live behaviour.
8. **Rota dependency.** Without the rota work, "responsible doctor" is only the deciding doctor, and handover is not visible.
