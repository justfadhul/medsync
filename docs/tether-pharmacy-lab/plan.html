What "connected to Tether" means

Today the Tether address sends pharmacy and lab staff away. After this, it keeps them.

Signs in on TetherA pharmacist, dispenser, lab technologist or lab in-charge signs in on the Tether address.
→
Lands on their workspacePharmacy roles go to /pharmacy and lab roles to /lab, the same rule the clinic floor uses for nurses and doctors (ADR 0509). Today these addresses are closed on Tether (ADR 0499), so staff are sent to MedSync and must sign in again.
→
Same pages, Tether frameThe navy rail, user dock, Emergency button and dialogs come from the existing DeskShell. Inside it the workspace's own places, tabs and pages run unchanged.

Migrations

None new

The frame and the landing are code. The lab's "On the rota" chip reads the duty-rota tables from migration 0307, which is already GATED and yours to apply. Until it is applied and DUTY_ROTA is on, the chip is simply not shown. Nothing writes to the database that doesn't already.

Flags

TETHER_LAB and TETHER_PHARMACY

Each takes 1 or a list of hospital ids, like WARD_BUILDER. You chose all hospitals, so each is set to 1. Off means the Tether address keeps sending these staff to MedSync, exactly as today. The MedSync address serves the same pages in its own frame either way, so the switch can be turned off without losing anything.

Build steps

Five small steps. Each one is checked and committed, and you review it before the next starts.

1

Switches, address and landing

The ADR, the two flags, /lab and /pharmacy opening on Tether when their flag is on, and pharmacy and lab roles landing on their workspace. A hospital not covered by the flag is still sent to MedSync. Covered by unit tests on tetherClosed and tetherHome.

2

Lab in the desk frame

LabDeskFrame: the lab's places and counts in the rail, with Scan or search and Needs you in the top bar. On Tether it replaces LabShellV2's own chrome, so there is never a double sidebar (ADR 0514). Checked in the browser at desktop, tablet and phone width, in light and dark.

3

Pharmacy in the desk frame

PharmacyDeskFrame does the same for the pharmacy, keeping the offline outbox banner, Find and Needs you. Hospitals on the older pharmacy pages get the same frame around those pages.

4

"On the rota" chip

Lab reads the duty rota's posts in the Laboratory department. Pharmacy reads its own roster's duties. Both are read only and say "rostered, not confirmed present". The lab in-charge, as head of the Laboratory department, makes the lab rota with the duty-rota editor that already exists (ADR 0534, D8).

5

Checks, then the PR on your yes

Typecheck, lint, the full test run and a build, then one commit per step on claude/tether-pharmacy-lab-1f10a7. I open the PR only when you say so.

Decisions for you

Four questions, each with my recommendation.

D1. How much of the Tether look?
RecommendedThe desk frame around the real pages, as shown above (the ADR 0520 pattern).
Or: open the addresses on Tether but keep each workspace's current header. It's smaller, but it wouldn't look like the rest of Tether.
D2. Does the pharmacy roster move into the duty rota?
RecommendedNo. The pharmacy roster stays the source, because it decides who may dispense at which point and the duty rota was copied from it. The lab, which has no rota today, uses the duty rota.
Or: move the pharmacy into duty posts. That needs a migration and a data move, and it touches a working dispensing rule.
D3. Can wards send care requests to the pharmacy?
RecommendedNot in this build. ADR 0533 (R32) keeps every pharmacist out of request routing in v1, and you left that decision open. The lab keeps criticals as its channel to wards.
Or: open "Ask pharmacy" as a separate, later piece with its own ADR and your clinical sign-off.
D4. A department calendar like the ward desks?
RecommendedLater. The ward desks' calendar exists only in the prototypes, with nothing behind it in the code. Building one here first would mean placeholder data.
Or: build the calendar's data model now, for every department, as its own piece of work.
Risks and how they're handled

Three things to know before switching it on

  • Offline pharmacy items stay with their address. The pharmacy's offline outbox is stored in the browser for each address. A hand-over queued offline on the MedSync address is sent the next time someone opens the pharmacy there; it does not appear on Tether. Turn the flag on at a quiet time, after the pharmacy screen shows 0 waiting for offline sync.
  • One sign-in per address. Staff who keep MedSync bookmarks still reach the same pages there. On Tether they sign in once more, because sign-in cookies belong to each address.
  • Wording is draft. The new labels (frame, chip, ADR) stay marked draft until you sign them. No clinical threshold, dose or rule changes in this work.
