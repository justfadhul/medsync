Hand-offs between cadres
  • 1Doctor requests two cannulas or raises the insulin → Nurse “Lines in”, “Set the pump”
  • 2Targets missed → Nurse checks lines and pumps → Doctor “Increase insulin” unlocks
  • 3Doctor signs and sends → Pharmacy order with live stock
  • 4Pharmacy hands over → Nurse medicine status
  • 5Nurse saves the hour and vitals → trends, targets and the flow chart re-route; glucose under 4 raises an urgent hypo task
  • 6Chart results decide resolution
  • 7Doctor prescribes for the cause, then SC insulin or a variable-rate infusion
  • 8Doctor ends the chart → nurse tasks clear
