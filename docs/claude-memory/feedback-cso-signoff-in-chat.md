The owner is the CSO. When they sign something off in chat during development, mark that content signed in the code so it opens to the people who use it. They will not sign again in the app.

Operational rules (order parameters, ward lists, other packs) are signed by the pharmacy in charge alone, in one tap, with no CSO, no second signer and no password-and-code prompt. Owner ruling, 31 Oct 2026.

**Why:** the owner finds the CSO gates and multi-signer flows exhausting and confusing, and has ADHD, so long flows and long messages cost them a lot.

**How to apply:**

- Never add an in-app CSO step.
- Ask for sign-off in chat as one short yes/no question.
- Keep app flows to the fewest taps.
- Keep messages to the owner short.
