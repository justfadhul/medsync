# Clerking → diabetes pack: rulings for the Clinical Safety Officer

ADR 0216. `lib/ncd/pack/from-clerking.ts` turns the ten-step clerking record into the pack's input. Most of it is a
plain rename (the record asks the pack's question). The calls below are clinical judgement; each is marked `MR-nn`
in the code and can be changed there in one place. Two laws hold throughout and are not up for ruling: a blank or
unasked answer is never "no", and nothing is invented.

Status of every ruling: **signed by the CSO, 2026-10-08, as proposed** (one bulk signature: "Accept all as proposed").
